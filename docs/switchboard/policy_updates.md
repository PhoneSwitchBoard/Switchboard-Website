## Policy and Terms Updates

This page summarises each change to the SwitchBoard Cloud™ [Privacy Policy](https://www.switchboard.cloud/privacy), [Terms of Service](https://www.switchboard.cloud/terms) and [Security Policy](https://www.switchboard.cloud/security), newest first. The pages on switchboard.cloud are the official text. This summary helps you see what changed and why.

For a material change to the Terms of Service, we give at least 30 days' notice by email or in SwitchBoard, and the change takes effect at the start of your next renewal term (on the stated date for free trials). We also tell customers before a new service provider starts handling their data.

## SwitchBoard 1.21

**Takes effect:** [date]

SwitchBoard 1.21 adds answer extraction, the Ask SwitchBoard assistant and OpenAI as a second provider for AI agents. The policies now describe them. They also name every provider a customer can choose to connect, including ones that SwitchBoard 1.20 could already use.

None of these providers receives your data unless you switch the feature on and connect your own account.

### Privacy Policy

- **What we process for you** (section 1) now includes the answers extracted from your calls, the questions your staff ask Ask SwitchBoard, and what AI agents collect on calls.
- **Service providers** (section 5):
  - The AI providers you can choose are OpenAI, Anthropic and Amazon Web Services (Transcribe, Bedrock, Comprehend and Polly). The list now covers text-to-speech, answer extraction and Ask SwitchBoard, with the data each one receives.
  - AI agents can run at ElevenLabs or OpenAI. The list says what they receive during a call.
  - Contact synchronisation now covers Intuit QuickBooks Online as well as Clio.
  - Automation platforms, currently Zapier, are listed with the call details they receive.
  - An AI engine that you host yourself is not our service provider.
- **How long we keep data** (section 8): Ask SwitchBoard's activity log is kept for the period you choose, 90 days unless you change it.
- **If you call or work for a SwitchBoard customer** (section 12): the organisation decides whether answer extraction, Ask SwitchBoard or AI agents are used. Callers may be speaking to an AI agent, and telling them is the organisation's responsibility.

### Terms of Service

- **Call features** (section 8) now include answer extraction, Ask SwitchBoard and AI agents. If you use an AI agent, you are responsible for telling callers they are speaking to one, for any consent needed to record or transcribe, and for what the agent says.
- **Features that send data to third parties** (section 9) now include answer extraction, Ask SwitchBoard, AI agents, accounting systems and automation platforms.
- **Automated output** (section 14): extracted answers and Ask SwitchBoard's answers are generated by software and can be wrong or incomplete, like transcripts and summaries. Check the underlying recording or record where accuracy matters.

### Security Policy

No change.
